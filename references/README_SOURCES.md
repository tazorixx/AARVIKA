# Reference source guide

- `developer_category_hierarchy_spec.pdf` is the exact 10-page source for 10 Hampers groups, 11 Stationery groups, 9 Personalized Gifts groups, and build-your-own combo product eligibility, durable goods and no-duplicate-product rules.
- `Furniture_Master_Map.png` includes 9 home and 9 commercial families with many grouped subtypes and special quote/design workflow.
- `Clothing_Category_Map.jpeg`, `Birthday_Map_1.jpeg`, `Birthday_Map_2.jpeg`, `Anniversary_Map.jpeg`, `AARVIKA_Purple_Home_Reference.png` encode desired browsing and visual language. Verify headings in original images before implementing.
- `Gift4U_Indian_Wedding_Taxonomy_Research.xlsx` and `Gift4U_Wedding_Product_Categorization_Complete.xlsx` are RESEARCH catalogues; not verified religious requirements, live inventory or purchasable products.
- `13scripts_PARTIAL_SOURCE_READONLY.zip` is an incomplete snapshot of old admin/source; **do not run as standalone production app**. `AARVIKA_13scripts_Admin_Audit.md` documents source mismatches to review at the admin phase, NOT to fix before app-complete gate.
- `marketplace-planning.zip` is Amazon observational research for dense/commercial UI and workflows. It does not prove Amazon pixel sizes or grant product image rights.
